16.3M+ Records Warehoused

SPARCS Healthcare & BI System

Problem: Public hospital discharge data arrives as separate yearly files, which makes multi-year clinical and financial trends slow and inconsistent to analyze.
Solution: Built a layered PostgreSQL warehouse (staging → data-quality validation → ETL → star schema) with 8 dimensions and 1 fact table, plus analytics views and materialized views for heavy aggregations.
Impact: One governed source of truth across 7 years of records, feeding an 8-page Power BI dashboard for hospital, patient, clinical and financial analysis.

  • 16.3M+ discharge records across 7 years
  • Star schema: 8 dimensions, 1 fact table
  • ETL with audit logging and post-load validation
  • 11 analytics views, 6 materialized views
  • EXPLAIN ANALYZE-driven query tuning
  • 8-page Power BI dashboard

Medical Inventory Forecasting & Decision Support System

Problem: Medical inventory is planned by hand, so hospitals either overstock (tying up capital) or run short on life-saving stock.
Solution: Benchmarked 14 regression models and ensembles, selected a tuned Gradient Boosting model, explained its predictions with SHAP, and segmented items with K-Means for procurement planning.
Impact: Shipped as a deployed multi-page Streamlit app that flags inventory risk and recommends procurement actions, with every prediction logged to a Google Sheet audit trail.

  • 14 models benchmarked and tuned
  • SHAP global and per-prediction explanations
  • K-Means inventory segmentation
  • Demand and inventory-risk flags
  • Procurement recommendation logic
  • Live Streamlit app with audit logging

21% Lower Cold-Start RMSE

Multimodal Cold-Start Movie Recommender

Problem: Collaborative filtering cannot rate movies that have little or no interaction history, so new releases are invisible to the recommender.
Solution: Combined Neural Collaborative Filtering with CLIP poster embeddings and VGGish trailer-audio embeddings, plus a dedicated cold-start model that recommends movies never seen in training.
Impact: On 2,107 movies unseen during training, the cold-start model reduced RMSE by about 21% against a global-mean baseline on the same test subset, and the overall multimodal model improved on a user-and-movie bias baseline.

  • 19,232 multimodal movies (MovieLens 20M)
  • 2,107 test movies unseen in training
  • CLIP poster and VGGish audio embeddings
  • Neural Collaborative Filtering
  • Dedicated cold-start model
  • Top-N personalized recommendations

2.75M+ Telemetry Rows

Data Center Resource Analytics

Problem: Data centers use large amounts of electricity and water for cooling, but the raw sensor telemetry does not show how workload turns into heat and water demand.
Solution: Profiled and cleaned 2.7M+ server readings, engineered workload and thermal features, and combined thermodynamic relationships (heat load, cooling-water flow, evaporative water) with a Linear Regression model after removing leakage-prone features.
Impact: Produced a data-driven framework linking computing workload to electricity, heat and cooling-water requirements, with the model reaching R² of 0.98 on held-out data.

  • 2.75M+ telemetry records profiled
  • Physics-based heat and water modeling
  • Leakage-free feature design
  • Hour-of-day cyclical encoding
  • Held-out R² of 0.98 (RMSE 0.013)
  • Correlation checks independent of the model

105K Decisions Modeled

Human Decision Intelligence

Problem: Observing how people choose under risk and ambiguity does not explain which factors drive those choices, or whether they can be predicted.
Solution: Ran mixed-effects statistical modeling, PCA and behavioral clustering on the CPC18 dataset, then compared GLM, GAM, Random Forest and XGBoost, with SHAP and LIME for explanations.
Impact: The final model reached 70.95% test accuracy and 0.79 ROC-AUC on 105,000 held-out decisions, and the findings are packaged as an R Markdown report series and a Shiny app.

  • 105,000 held-out test decisions
  • 70.95% test accuracy, 0.79 ROC-AUC
  • GLM, GAM, Random Forest and XGBoost compared
  • PCA and behavioral clustering
  • SHAP and LIME explanations
  • Mixed-effects statistical analysis
